**Third Place Japan runs two separate systems. TPJ Select is an editorial pick — spaces our team finds and writes up on our own judgment, with no application from the venue involved. Certification (Certified, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Flagship) is the opposite: a formal, five-grade system a venue applies for and is assessed against. Both exist to do the same underlying job — find Japan's genuine third places and make their value legible — but they do it through different mechanisms, on purpose.**

New readers often ask why certification alone wasn't enough — why add a second, unpaid track on top of it. The answer is straightforward: discovery and assessment are two different jobs. Force them into a single system and both end up compromised.

---

## TPJ Select: An Editorial Pick, No Application Required

**TPJ Select highlights spaces our editorial team has found and finds genuinely interesting right now — no application from the venue is involved.**

The bar for inclusion is narrow but specific: a space needs to score well on at least five of TPJ's seven axes, with a top score on either specialness or story as the one non-negotiable line. Every certified venue page, TPJ Select included, publishes its score out of 10 across all seven axes; a radar chart and expanded amenity detail are reserved for Platinum grade and above. Keeping the assessment layer light on TPJ Select is a deliberate choice — it protects the "our team found this" story that the tier is built on, rather than turning it into a transaction.

Large chain operators and listed-company house brands don't appear in TPJ Select, for a related reason: once a brand is that large, "our editorial team found this" stops being a credible claim. For the fuller picture, see [TPJ Select](/stories/select/) *(Japanese-language listing; an English version is not yet available)*.

## Certification: A Formal, Five-Grade System Built on Application

**Certification — Certified, Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Flagship — is a formal, five-tier listing a venue applies for and is evaluated against.**

Unlike TPJ Select, certification starts with the venue's own consent and application. The same seven axes get scored out of 10, but as a grade climbs, the depth of what's published — structured data, amenity detail, tag count — opens up in stages. Certified is the entry point; Flagship is the top tier. The distinctions between grades are broken down in [TPJ's certification grades](/stories/about/certification-grades/) *(Japanese article; an English version is in progress)*.

## Why Keep Them Separate: Discovery and Assessment Are Different Jobs

**TPJ Select and certification stay separate because discovery and assessment call for different things.**

Discovery runs on editorial judgment — it needs speed and flexibility. Assessment runs on a venue's own intent to apply and a numeric check against seven axes — it needs consistency and rigor. Try to run both through one system and the two stories undercut each other: "our team found this" and "this venue applied and passed" don't sit well in the same sentence.

The path from TPJ Select into certification is built in as a natural next step, not an afterthought. If the operator of a space TPJ Select has already featured wants formal recognition, applying for Certified grade or above is the route. Discovery comes first, assessment later — that ordering is the design logic behind the whole system.

---

## What It Means for a Venue

For a space TPJ Select features, that inclusion is a quiet form of recognition — being noticed, on the strength of the space itself. Certification means something different: the operator chose to put the space forward and asked to be measured against TPJ's standard. Neither ranks above the other. They describe two different relationships to the process.

## In Summary

TPJ Select and certification are deliberately kept apart because they serve two different functions: discovery and assessment. TPJ Select is an unpaid, editorially curated pick; certification is a formal five-grade system built on a venue's own application. Running both, rather than forcing one system to do both jobs, is what lets Third Place Japan evaluate the structure of a space — not simply tally reviews — at any scale, from a single-room café to a flagship hotel.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Is TPJ Select a lower tier than certification?**<br>
No — it's not a ranking. TPJ Select is an unpaid, editorially curated pick; certification is a formal system built on a venue's own application. They measure different things. What they share is the same underlying goal: finding Japan's genuine third places and making their value legible.

**Q. Can a space featured in TPJ Select go on to get certified?**<br>
Yes. If the operator of a TPJ Select space wants formal recognition, applying for Certified grade or above is the next step, and that path is built into the system by design.

**Q. Why doesn't TPJ Select accept applications from venues?**<br>
Because doing so would change what the tier actually is. The moment a venue can apply, "our editorial team found this" turns into "this venue submitted itself" — a different claim, and a weaker one for what TPJ Select is trying to do.

**Q. Does certification cost anything?**<br>
Certified grade is a free application. For Silver grade and above, inquiries go through the main site's For Business page.

**Q. Do TPJ Select and certification use different evaluation criteria?**<br>
No — both run on the same seven-axis framework, and every certified venue page, TPJ Select included, publishes scores out of 10 on all seven. What differs is how much additional detail gets published: a radar chart and fuller amenity data appear only from Platinum grade upward.
